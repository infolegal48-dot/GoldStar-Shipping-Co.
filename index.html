How We Handle Your Assets

Assaying, Melting & Packaging

A closer look at the processes behind every consignment we accept.

Assaying

Verifying Purity & Weight

Every shipment is assayed on intake to confirm purity, weight, and authenticity before it enters our custody chain.

  • Independent lab verification of metal purity or gemstone grading
  • Certified weight and quality documentation issued to the client
  • Results recorded against the shipment before transport begins
Melting

Controlled Processing

Where melting or re-casting is requested, it is carried out in a controlled, monitored environment by qualified handlers.

  • Supervised melting with continuous weight reconciliation
  • Re-casting into client-specified bar or ingot formats
  • Full handling log retained for every batch processed
Custom Packaging

Options & Benefits

Packaging is tailored to the value and sensitivity of each shipment for maximum protection in transit.

  • Tamper-evident sealing and reinforced casing options
  • Discreet, unmarked exterior packaging on request
  • Climate and impact protection for sensitive gemstones

Licensed & Compliant. Meridian Vault Freight is licensed by the government to transport and handle precious metals and gemstones, and operates in accordance with all applicable regulatory requirements.
